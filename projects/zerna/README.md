# Zerna

> Minimalist commerce with effortless management.

## Overview

Zerna is an e-commerce platform focused on delivering a clean, modern shopping interface coupled with an intuitive store administrative experience. The platform eliminates visual clutter and operational friction, allowing merchants to focus on catalog presentation, inventory health, and transaction fulfillment.

Built to streamline the path from browsing to checkout, Zerna pairs an uncluttered digital storefront with proven backend management workflows. The platform ensures seamless customer interactions across desktop and mobile devices while providing administrators with clear operational oversight.

With integrated WooCommerce-powered administration and native Razorpay transaction handling, Zerna delivers reliable store configuration, order tracking, and product management in a lightweight footprint.

## Purpose

Online store management often becomes hindered by complex, bloated software architectures and crowded user interfaces that distract shoppers and complicate daily store operations. 

Zerna solves this problem by offering a minimalist digital storefront optimized for speed, legibility, and effortless administrative control, enabling merchants to launch and maintain their retail operations without operational overhead.
