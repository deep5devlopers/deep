# Grexon

> Simple and intelligent navigation for indoor spaces.

## Overview

Grexon is a web-based indoor navigation platform engineered to assist visitors, staff, and facilities personnel in discovering locations and navigating complex architectural environments. The platform provides intuitive visual maps and point-to-point routing directly through modern web browsers without requiring dedicated hardware installations or native mobile application downloads.

Designed for large-scale facilities such as medical centers, academic campuses, transit terminals, and commercial complexes, Grexon translates complex building layouts into readable, interactive digital floor plans. Users can search for rooms, administrative offices, emergency amenities, and specialized departments with immediate route visualization.

The platform emphasizes ease of access, accessible route computation, and scalable spatial data organization to accommodate facilities with multi-building and multi-floor configurations.

## Purpose

Navigating large, multi-story indoor facilities is often disorienting, causing visitor frustration, missed appointments, and operational bottlenecks for facility support personnel. Traditional static signage is difficult to update and lacks personalized wayfinding context.

Grexon addresses indoor wayfinding challenges by providing accessible, browser-based indoor maps with responsive search and dynamic route guidance that guides users directly to their desired destination.

## Product Status

**Beta Testing**: The platform web navigation interface, floor plan rendering pipeline, and indoor pathfinding algorithms are currently undergoing beta testing across designated facility environments.

## Technology & Architecture

The architecture incorporates vector-based indoor map rendering, client-side spatial indexing, graph-based shortest path routing calculation, and a cloud-hosted spatial database for structural floor plan management.

## Roadmap

- Accessible step-free routing options for wheelchair and mobility-impaired visitors
- Multi-building campus connector navigation and outdoor-to-indoor transition routing
- Facility management portal for dynamic room status and temporary detour updates
- QR-code point-of-interest markers for instant current-location wayfinding initialization

## Vision

To make complex indoor environments universally accessible, discoverable, and effortless to navigate for anyone on any device.
